Nonprofit Fundraising & Development

Everything you need to navigate the course, track your progress, use your Excel toolkits, and get the most out of every lesson and quiz.

1. Introduction

The Nonprofit Fundraising & Development course is a comprehensive, self-paced learning platform covering every major area of nonprofit fundraising — from foundational principles and donor psychology to major gifts, grants, planned giving, and capital campaigns.

The course includes 10 modules, each containing multiple lessons, a comprehension quiz, and a practical Excel toolkit to apply what you've learned. All your progress, notes, and activity responses are saved automatically to your browser so you can pick up right where you left off.

Creating an Account

1
Open the course The sign-in screen appears automatically. Click Sign Up to switch to the registration form.
2
Enter your name and email Use a valid email address — this is your account identifier and must be unique.
3
Create a password Minimum 6 characters. Re-enter it to confirm.
4
Team code (optional) If your organization has a team, enter the team code here. You can also join a team later from the dashboard.
5
Accept terms & click "Create Free Account" You are signed in immediately and taken to the dashboard.

Signing In

On the sign-in screen, enter your registered email and password, then click Sign In. Your progress, notes, and streak are restored automatically.

Account storage: All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Accounts are tied to the device and browser you use. Clearing browser data or switching browsers will reset your progress.

Signing Out

Click Sign Out in the top-right header bar at any time to log out. Your progress is saved automatically before sign-out.

2. Dashboard Overview

After signing in, you land on the Dashboard — your home base. It shows your overall progress, learning streak, and tabs for navigating modules, the glossary, and the resource library.

Click the BYC logo and course title in the header at any time to return to the Dashboard from anywhere in the app.

Overall Progress Bar

The blue progress bar beneath the hero banner shows your percentage of lessons and quizzes completed across all 10 modules. It updates every time you complete a lesson or pass a quiz. When it reaches 100%, your Certificate of Completion becomes available.

Stats Strip

Three stat cards sit below the progress bar:

StatWhat it shows
🔥 Current StreakConsecutive days with at least one completed lesson. Completing a lesson today extends your streak. Missing a day resets it to zero.
🏆 Best StreakYour all-time longest streak — a personal record that persists even after a streak is broken.
⏰ Content RemainingApproximate time left to finish all uncompleted lessons, based on each lesson's listed duration.
Streak tip: The streak icon changes color as you build it — 3+ days turns orange (💥), 7+ days turns flame (🔥). Complete at least one lesson per day to keep your streak alive.

Dashboard Tabs

Three tabs organize the main dashboard content:

TabContent

Each module card on the dashboard shows a color-coded accent bar, the module level, and a mini progress bar showing how many of its lessons and quizzes you've completed.

Module Detail View

Click any module card to open it. You'll see:

  • Module header — title, description, difficulty level, lesson count, and estimated time
  • Learning Objectives — the skills you'll develop in this module
  • Lesson list — all lessons and the module quiz, each showing completion status
  • Toolkit download (where available) — a green download box to grab the Excel toolkit for that module

Lessons

Click any lesson row to open the Lesson Player. Each lesson contains:

  • Content tab — the full lesson text with callout boxes, comparison tables, and practical examples
  • Notes tab — a personal note-taking area. Notes are saved automatically per lesson.
  • Activity tab — a reflective exercise to apply the lesson to your organization. Responses are saved per lesson.

Use the ← Prev and Next → buttons at the top to move between lessons without returning to the module view. The current lesson number and module name are shown in the breadcrumb trail.

Marking complete: Click "Mark as Complete" at the bottom of any lesson to log it as done. The lesson row turns green and your progress bar updates. Completing all lessons and the module quiz marks the full module as complete.

Quizzes

Each module ends with a 5-question multiple-choice quiz. Quizzes are found at the bottom of each module's lesson list, labeled with a 📝 quiz badge.

When you take a quiz:

  • Select your answer for each question, then click Submit Quiz
  • Correct answers are shown in green, incorrect answers in red
  • Each question includes an explanation that appears after submission
  • Your score (e.g., "4/5 — 80%") is shown at the top
  • A score of 4/5 or higher counts the quiz as passed and marks it complete
Re-taking quizzes: You can retake any quiz at any time. Only a passing score (4/5+) marks the quiz as complete in your progress tracking.

Notes & Activities

The Notes tab in each lesson lets you jot down reflections, quotes, or action items. Click Save Notes to store them — a confirmation message appears briefly. Notes persist between sessions.

The Activity tab presents a practical exercise tied to the lesson content. Type your response in the text area and click Save Response. Activities are stored per-lesson and can be revisited and updated at any time.

Excel Toolkits

Several modules include a downloadable Excel Toolkit — a professional-grade spreadsheet designed to help you apply that module's concepts at your organization. The toolkit appears as a green download box at the top of the module detail view.

Click "Download Toolkit (Excel)" to save the file to your computer. Each toolkit opens in Microsoft Excel or any compatible spreadsheet application.

See Section 10 — Toolkit Reference for the complete list of available toolkits and what each contains.

Lesson Search

On the Course Modules tab, a search bar sits above the module grid. Type any keyword to instantly search all lesson titles across all 10 modules. Results show the lesson title, module, level, and duration. Click Go → or press Enter to jump directly to that lesson.

Click the ✕ button in the search bar to clear the search and return to the full module grid.

4. Glossary

The Glossary tab (on the dashboard) contains over 60 fundraising terms defined in plain, practical language — from Annual Fund and Bequest to SYBUNT and Unrestricted Gift.

Searching the Glossary

Type any word into the search bar at the top of the Glossary tab. The list filters in real time to show matching terms and definitions. Clear the search to see all terms.

Browsing by Letter

Click any letter button in the alphabet strip to jump to terms beginning with that letter. Click the active letter again (or click a new letter) to navigate. Clicking "All" resets the filter to show every term.

Glossary Entry Format

Each entry shows the term, its abbreviation (if applicable), and a full definition. Some key terms are referenced directly in lesson content throughout the course.

6. Streak Tracking

The streak system encourages daily learning by tracking consecutive days on which you complete at least one lesson or quiz.

TermMeaning
Current StreakThe number of consecutive days you've completed at least one lesson. Resets to zero if you miss a day.
Best StreakYour personal record — the longest streak you've ever achieved. Preserved even when a current streak breaks.
Streak ActiveIf you've completed a lesson today, the streak card turns orange and shows "Active today ✓".

The streak icon changes as your streak grows:

  • 0 days: — (no streak yet)
  • 1–2 days: 🔥 (starting out)
  • 3–6 days: 💥 (building momentum)
  • 7+ days: 🔥 (on fire!)
Streak data is stored locally in your browser. It will not carry over if you switch browsers or clear your browser's local storage.

7. Certificate of Completion

When you complete all 10 modules — meaning every lesson is marked complete and every quiz has a passing score — a congratulations banner appears on your dashboard and your Certificate of Completion becomes available.

Viewing Your Certificate

Click "View & Print Your Certificate" from the completion banner or the certificate button that appears in the header. A full-page certificate overlay opens showing:

  • Your full name (from your account)
  • Course title: Nonprofit Fundraising & Development
  • Completion date
  • Build Your Club seal and signature line

Printing Your Certificate

Click "🖨 Print / Save PDF" in the certificate overlay. Your browser's print dialog opens. Select "Save as PDF" to save a digital copy, or choose a physical printer to print it. For best results, use landscape orientation and enable "Background graphics" in your print settings.

Click "✕ Close" to dismiss the certificate and return to your dashboard.

Certificate tip: Your name on the certificate comes directly from your account's First Name + Last Name fields. Make sure you entered your full name during registration to ensure the certificate looks correct.

8. Team Enrollment

The Team Enrollment feature allows nonprofit staff teams to learn together. A Team Leader creates a team and shares a team code; Team Members join using that code. Team Leaders can view their team's learning progress from a dedicated team dashboard.

Becoming a Team Leader

1
Sign in to the course with your account.
2
Look for the team prompt on your dashboard. If you are not yet part of a team, a call-to-action card appears offering you the option to create one.
3
Click "Create a Team" and enter a Team Name (e.g., your organization's name). Click Create.
4
Your Team Code is generated automatically. Share this 6-character code with your colleagues so they can join.
5
Access your Team Dashboard via the 👥 My Team button in the header.

Your Team Code

Your team code is a short, unique identifier visible at the top of your Team Dashboard. Share it with anyone you want to add to your team. Each team has one leader and can have unlimited members.

Joining a Team as a Member

1
Get the team code from your Team Leader.
2
During signup, enter the team code in the Team Code field on the registration form.
3
Alternatively, if already registered, the dashboard shows a "Join a Team" prompt if you are not yet on a team. Enter your team code there.
4
You're added to the team immediately. Your progress will now appear in your Team Leader's team dashboard.

👥 My Team Page (Team Leaders)

Team Leaders see a 👥 My Team button in the header after creating a team. This page shows:

  • Your team name and team code
  • Each team member's name, email, lessons completed, modules completed, and current streak
  • A sortable summary table of all member progress
Note: Team Leaders can see member progress, but cannot view member notes or quiz answers — only completion counts and streaks.

9. Admin Panel

The Admin Panel is accessible to users with the admin role. Admins see an Admin button in the header after signing in.

Accessing the Admin Panel

Click the Admin button in the top-right header. The Admin Panel opens, showing a full overview of all registered users and their course activity.

Summary Statistics

Four stat cards at the top of the Admin Panel show:

  • Total Users — all registered accounts
  • Admin Accounts — users with admin privileges
  • Active Streaks — users who completed a lesson today
  • Avg. Lessons Completed — mean lessons finished across all users

User Cards & Search

Below the stats, every registered user appears as a row in a searchable table. Each row shows:

  • Name and email
  • Account role (Admin or Student)
  • Lessons completed and modules completed
  • Current streak (days)
  • A Delete button (with confirmation) to remove a user account

Use the search bar at the top of the user table to filter by name or email in real time.

Exporting User Data

Click "Export CSV" to download a comma-separated file containing all user data (name, email, role, lessons completed, modules completed, streak). The file can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets for reporting.

Module Completion Rates

A second table in the Admin Panel shows each module's completion rate — the percentage of users who have completed every lesson and quiz in that module — helping you identify which content is most and least completed across your user base.

Administrator Access

The sign-in screen has an Administrator Access link below the Sign In button. Use it to sign in as Administrator with just a password — no email needed. This is a per-browser admin role; the password is stored only on the current computer.

  • First time: Click Administrator Access. You'll see a "First-time setup" prompt with two password fields — enter a password (6+ characters) and confirm it. Click Create Admin Password.
  • Subsequent times: Click Administrator Access, enter that same password, and click Enter Admin Panel.
  • Once signed in as Administrator, you'll land on the dashboard with full admin privileges, including the Admin page in the sidebar (visibility into all teams, users, and activity stored in this browser).
  • Click ← Back to regular sign-in at the bottom of the admin panel to return to the normal email/password form.

Note: the admin password is unique to each browser. If you set it up at home and then visit the app on a work computer, you'll see the first-time-setup prompt again.

11. FAQ & Troubleshooting

My progress was reset. What happened?
All course data is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser history/cache, switching browsers, or using a private/incognito window will result in a fresh start. Always use the same browser and user profile to preserve your progress.
Can I use the course on multiple devices?
Because data is stored locally (not in a cloud account), progress does not sync across devices. Use one consistent browser on one device for the best experience.
I completed a quiz but my module doesn't show as complete.
A module is marked complete only when all lessons AND the quiz are marked done. Make sure every lesson row shows a green checkmark, and that your quiz score was 4/5 or higher.
The quiz won't let me submit. What do I do?
You must answer all 5 questions before the Submit button becomes active. Scroll through the quiz to ensure no question was skipped.
My streak disappeared even though I used the course yesterday.
Streaks require completing (marking done) at least one lesson per day — simply opening a lesson or reading it does not count. Click "Mark as Complete" at the bottom of a lesson to register it.
The Excel toolkit file won't open.
Ensure the file downloaded completely (check your Downloads folder). Open it in Microsoft Excel 2016+, Google Sheets (File → Import → Upload), or LibreOffice Calc. If Excel shows a security warning, click "Enable Editing."
I don't see the Admin button even though I'm an admin.
The Admin button only appears for accounts with the admin role. If you should have admin access, contact your course administrator to verify your account role in the user data.
My team members aren't appearing on my Team Dashboard.
Make sure your team members entered the correct team code when registering or joining. Team codes are case-sensitive. Ask members to check their account and confirm they see their team name on the dashboard.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Passwords are stored locally in your browser. If you've forgotten yours, you can create a new account with the same email address — note that this will create a separate progress record. Contact your administrator if you need to merge accounts.
My certificate shows the wrong name.
Your certificate pulls your name from the First Name and Last Name fields you entered at registration. These fields are set at signup and cannot be edited after account creation in the current version of the app.
The lesson search isn't finding what I'm looking for.
The search bar on the Course Modules tab searches lesson titles only. For content inside lessons, navigate to the relevant module and use your browser's built-in Find function (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) once you're inside a lesson.
